# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing the project. Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through the contact details on the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical. The maintainer may remove or edit contributions, issue a warning, or exclude a person temporarily or permanently from project spaces based on the conduct and its impact.
